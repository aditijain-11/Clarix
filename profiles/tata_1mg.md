# Business Profile: Tata 1mg

## Overview
- **Name:** Tata 1mg (formerly 1mg, formerly rooted in HealthKart Plus)
- **Industry / category:** E-pharmacy and digital healthcare
- **Founded / ownership:** 2015 as "1mg"; became Tata 1mg in 2021 after the Tata Group acquired a majority stake and folded it into Tata Digital
- **HQ and markets served:** Gurugram; most of India
- **Mission:** Make healthcare more accessible, understandable, and affordable
- **One-liner:** Online pharmacy and digital healthcare platform where people in India buy medicines, book lab tests, consult doctors, and look up drug information in one place.

## Business lines
| Line | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Online pharmacy (flagship) | Rx + OTC medicines and health/wellness products, home delivery, usually discounted vs MRP | Search relevance is central: match query to exact drug, strength, salt, pack size across a huge catalog |
| Diagnostics / lab tests | Book blood tests and health checkups, home sample collection, digital reports | Own labs + partner network |
| E-consultation / teleconsultation | Online doctor advice and prescriptions | |
| Health information | Library of drug and disease content: uses, dosage, side effects, substitutes, interactions | Oldest part of the business; drives a lot of organic traffic |

## Differentiators
- Full-stack: pharmacy + diagnostics + consults + content
- Tata brand backing and trust

## Expansion arenas
- **Current arenas (per owner):** pharmacy supply, lab tests, digital health system. Main goal of the research: how 1mg can expand each of these.
- **Candidate adjacent arenas (hypotheses, to research):** AI tools for doctors (clinical decision support, patient management), chronic-care and medication adherence, preventive health and wellness, B2B supply to clinics and pharmacies
- **Assets we could reuse:** Tata brand trust, drug and disease content and SEO traffic, catalog and search data, lab reports and prescriptions, home-collection and delivery network, doctor network

## Watch focus
- **Product / quality lens:** search relevance and query matching, catalog quality, UX, app quality
- **Strategy lens:** pricing and discounts, growth, market share
- **Expansion lens:** how 1mg can grow into or deepen its arenas, including ideas from small innovative startups (example the owner gave: MedMitra AI)
- **Priority when they conflict:** whichever is likely to have the greater impact on our business performance
