## Early observations (hypotheses, not yet the analysis)
- Quick commerce is entering Rx and OTC, but Drugs and Cosmetics Act licensing is per store and slows scaling. Threat is highest for urgent and acute orders.
- Truemeds attacks on price for chronic and generic medicines.
- Apollo 24/7 digital is near breakeven while growing 23%, backed by 7,440 stores.
- PharmEasy is deleveraging via Thyrocare; diagnostics is its profit anchor, which puts pressure on 1mg's diagnostics line.
